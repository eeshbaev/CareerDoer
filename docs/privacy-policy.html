Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 24, 2026 · Last updated: July 24, 2026

Short version: CareerDoer keeps your data on your device. We do not require an account, we do not upload your answers to our servers, and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

CareerDoer (“we”, “us”) is a mobile career and wealth companion app. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

2. Information we process

CareerDoer may store the following on your device:

  • Profile details you enter (for example name, preferences, goals)
  • Answers from onboarding and career assessments
  • App settings (theme, language, sound preferences)
  • Progress and notes you create inside the app

This information is stored locally on your phone (for example in on-device storage). It is used only to personalize your CareerDoer experience on that device.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not require you to create an account
  • We do not sync your profile to CareerDoer cloud servers
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information
  • We do not use your assessment answers for advertising profiles

4. Internet access

The Android app may declare internet permission for platform/WebView operation. Your career profile and answers are designed to remain on-device. If a future update adds optional online features, we will update this policy before those features collect data off-device.

5. Analytics and crash reports

The current version of CareerDoer does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs that track you across apps. If that changes, this policy will be updated and the in-app experience will reflect the change.

6. Children

CareerDoer is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because data stays on your device, you control it. You can delete app data by clearing the app’s storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling CareerDoer. Uninstalling removes locally stored CareerDoer data from that device.

8. Your choices

  • Use the app without creating an account
  • Change or clear information inside the app where available
  • Clear app storage or uninstall to remove local data

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us at privacy@careerdoer.app (or replace this address with your support email before publishing).
